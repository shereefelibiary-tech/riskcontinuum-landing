Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Risk Continuum CKM ("RCCKM", "we", "us") is a clinical decision-support tool for clinicians. This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this website or use the RCCKM app, and how we handle it. RCCKM is in early access and this policy may change as the product develops; we will update the date above when it does.

Patient identifiers

RCCKM does not need patient identifiers. Leave out patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, addresses, contact details and other information that identifies a patient; the app flags them if it finds any. RCCKM is not currently offered as a HIPAA-covered service, and we do not sign business associate agreements at this time.

Chart text and worksheet values

  • RCCKM runs in your browser. Chart text you paste and the values in the worksheet are read and assessed on your device; they are not sent to our servers unless you choose to ask the optional Clinical Pearl (below).
  • Reports, notes, the patient roadmap and PDFs are also made on your device. They are held only in the open page and are cleared when you close or reload it, or after a period of inactivity. We do not save them.
  • We do not use chart text or worksheet values for advertising, and we do not sell them.

Optional AI features

The Clinical Pearl is optional and uses an outside AI provider. It sends nothing unless you ask: the app first shows the exact de-identified summary it would send (values, findings and recommendations, with no chart text, dates or patient identifiers), and sends it through our server to the provider only when you choose Send. Do not use AI features with identifiable information.

Accounts

If you create an account, we store the details you give us and basic account records: your name, email address, professional role and specialty, optional organization, country, sign-up and sign-in dates, account status, and when you accepted the clinical use notice. We use this to run your account, secure the service and contact you about it. Accounts never hold patient data or worksheet contents. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider using secure cookies.

Contact and feedback forms

If you send us a message through the contact or feedback form on this website or in the app, we store what you enter (your message, and any name, email address, organization or rating you give) along with the page you sent it from and the time. If you are signed in to the app, the message is linked to your account. We use these messages only to reply to you and to improve RCCKM, and we do not sell them. Please do not include patient information in a message. You can ask us to delete a message by emailing shereef@riskcontinuum.health.

Usage analytics

We use PostHog to understand visits to our website, pages and sections viewed, buttons used, time spent, account registration and sign-in, feature use, performance and technical errors. Before sign-in, these events use a random browser identifier stored in a cookie. After you sign in, we link activity to your account ID and profile details (name, email, professional role, specialty, organization and country), including visits before sign-in when the same browser identifier is available. We also collect referring website domains, recognized campaign sources, browser/device categories and approximate city, region and country derived from your IP address when location collection is enabled. Technical error records include predefined categories and app build information. Analytics never include passwords, verification tokens, pasted chart text, patient identifiers, clinical values, assessment results, report contents, or full URLs and query strings. We do not use advertising trackers or record clinical screens.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies that the service needs: to keep your worksheet session, to remember that you accepted the clinical use notice, to keep you signed in if you have an account, and the analytics identifiers described above. You can clear or block cookies in your browser; some features may then stop working.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to host the app, handle sign-in, measure usage and, when you choose AI features, generate AI responses. They process information only to provide those services to us.

Security

Connections to RCCKM are encrypted, account access is checked on the server, the assessment runs on your device, and we limit the information we collect to what the service needs. No system is perfectly secure, which is why RCCKM keeps patient data on your device and asks you to leave out identifiers.

Your choices

A free clinician account is required for the app workspace during early access. You can preview synthetic sample notes on the public website without signing in. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account information by emailing us.

Contact

Questions about privacy: shereef@riskcontinuum.health
