    How it works
    
From chart text to a plan in about a minute.

    

      
01 · 📋
Paste or enter
Paste de-identified chart text or your SmartPhrase, or type values straight into the worksheet. When you paste, a rule-based parser fills the worksheet and shows what it found for you to confirm.

      
02 · ⚡
Start Assessment
Position, PREVENT, signal score, trajectory, staging, diagnoses and actions in seconds, from a deterministic engine. The optional AI Clinical Pearl is labeled and kept separate.

      
03 · 🎚️
Try a plan
Tap interventions with your patient and watch the curve move. Add the plan you agree on to the note.

      
04 · ✅
Copy and hand off
Paste the note into the EMR and print the roadmap before the patient leaves.

        Meet the founder
        
Built by a physician. Grounded in clinical practice.

        
Shereef El-Ibiary, MD

        
Internal Medicine · Primary Care · Hospital Medicine · Population Health

        

        
We are at a remarkable moment in medicine. Advances in technology, treatments, and clinical evidence are opening new possibilities for prevention. Our patients’ records hold valuable information about their health and how it is changing. The opportunity now is to bring it all together, efficiently and meaningfully, at the point of care and beyond.

        
After nearly two decades caring for patients across primary care, hospital medicine, and population health, I built Risk Continuum CKM to help put that opportunity into practice. RCCKM connects a patient’s history, clinical findings, and trends with relevant evidence, helping clinicians see the whole picture and turn that understanding into an individualized approach to prevention.

        
My goal is to make comprehensive prevention easier to deliver in everyday practice. By bringing the information and evidence together, we can support clinician judgment, strengthen conversations with patients, and extend our attention from today’s decisions to long-term health.

        

        
Reconcile. Curate. Care.
